## Description

Art Flow Creative is a business management app built for artists who sell across marketplaces and need a simpler way to stay organized.

Keep your day-to-day business information together instead of switching between marketplaces, inboxes, notes, and separate trackers.

Key features include:

- Marketplace order and sales tracking
- Business expense and receipt tracking
- Inventory and product records
- Available and sold-item history
- Mileage tracking
- Reports and tax-planning organization
- Business planning and financial organization
- Backup and export tools
- Optional Gmail and Yahoo connections for importing supported marketplace sales and business receipt emails
- Native iPhone export sharing
- Account controls, including permanent in-app account deletion

Art Flow Creative is designed to help independent artists spend less time organizing business records and more time creating.

Some features require an auto-renewable subscription purchased through Apple. Available plans, localized prices, billing periods, and any eligible introductory offers are shown before purchase.

## App Review Notes

Art Flow Creative is a business-management app for independent artists and marketplace sellers.

A reviewer can use the supplied demo account without connecting any external marketplace or inbox. Core screens available after login include Dashboard, Orders, Expenses, Inventory/Products, Mileage, Reports, Business Planning, and Account.

The iOS app contains native Apple integrations in addition to its hosted business interface:
- StoreKit 2 auto-renewable subscriptions and entitlement checks
- Restore Purchases
- Manage Apple Subscription
- native iOS share sheet for Sales and Expense CSV exports
- native pull-to-refresh
- native loading/offline retry state
- external marketplace/support links open in the appropriate external iOS app
- camera/photo-library support for product and inventory images

Subscription products:
- com.artflowcreative.app.monthly
- com.artflowcreative.app.yearly

Any introductory trial eligibility and localized subscription price are provided by StoreKit.

Optional integrations:
- Gmail read-only connection for supported sales/receipt email importing
- Yahoo Mail connection for supported sales/receipt importing
- eBay and other marketplace-related business tracking

External integrations are not required to create an Art Flow account or inspect the core business-management interface.

Account deletion is available in Account → Delete Account. For a sole-owner workspace, associated owned Art Flow business data is removed before the login account is deleted. In a shared workspace, other linked users retain the workspace and the deleting user's access is removed.

Deleting an Art Flow account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Users can manage or cancel Apple billing through Apple's subscription management interface.
