# Art Flow Creative — Age Rating Worksheet

Prepared for the September 2026 App Store age-rating questionnaire.

Apple calculates the final rating from the answers entered in App Store Connect. This worksheet records what the current Art Flow code/product appears to contain so the questionnaire can be completed consistently.

## App purpose

Art Flow Creative is a business/productivity app for artists and independent sellers. Its primary functions are:
- order and sales tracking;
- business expenses;
- inventory/products;
- mileage;
- reports and tax-planning organization;
- business planning;
- optional marketplace/email connections;
- storefront/product management.

## Social media capability

**Recommended answer: No**

The current app does not provide:
- a social feed;
- public follower/following relationships;
- likes/reactions on other users’ content;
- comments on other users’ content;
- user-to-user direct messaging;
- reposting/resharing that amplifies user-generated content to a discovery feed;
- public ranking/trending of user-generated posts.

The native iOS Share Sheet lets a user export their own business CSV file to an external app. That is not an in-app social feed or social-media amplification feature.

The public storefront displays a seller’s products; it is a commerce/catalog experience, not a social feed.

## Content descriptors — current product

Based on the current business-management code, the app itself does not intentionally provide:
- cartoon/fantasy violence;
- realistic violence;
- prolonged graphic/sadistic violence;
- sexual content or nudity;
- profanity/crude humor;
- horror/fear themes;
- medical/treatment information;
- alcohol/tobacco/drug use;
- gambling/simulated gambling;
- contests/sweepstakes;
- loot boxes;
- weapons content;
- unrestricted social-media interaction.

**Important:** Sellers can upload their own product images and descriptions. Before answering the final questionnaire, confirm whether the submitted iOS experience exposes a public storefront containing seller-created artwork that could include mature imagery. If Art Flow controls no public discovery feed and only shows the signed-in seller’s own business/store content, answer based on the actual App Store Connect wording.

## Web access

Art Flow does not include a general-purpose browser.

The iOS wrapper:
- keeps Art Flow pages inside the app;
- sends user-activated external links to the appropriate external iOS app/browser;
- does not provide arbitrary URL entry or unrestricted web browsing.

## Commerce

The app can manage products/orders and may expose the Art Flow storefront, but it does not sell regulated goods by design.

Confirm marketplace/store policies before launch so prohibited/regulatory product categories are not intentionally facilitated.

## User-generated content

Users can create/upload:
- product/inventory images;
- titles/descriptions;
- notes;
- business plans;
- schedule/mileage records.

These are primarily private business records or seller storefront content, not a public social community.

If App Store Connect asks whether the app includes “User-Generated Content” as a broad capability, review Apple’s exact current definition before selecting the answer; do not equate private user-entered business data with a social feed automatically.

## Likely low-risk answers to verify in App Store Connect

For the current build, most objectionable-content frequency questions should likely be **None** unless the exact questionnaire defines seller-uploaded storefront artwork differently.

Capabilities likely absent:
- social media;
- messaging/chat;
- gambling;
- advertising;
- unrestricted browser;
- location sharing;
- dating;
- public random-user interaction.

## Before submitting

1. Complete the current age-rating questionnaire in App Store Connect.
2. Answer the Social Media capability question explicitly.
3. Review the public storefront content shown in the iOS build.
4. Do not choose a final age rating manually; let Apple calculate the rating from the questionnaire responses.
5. Revisit this worksheet if Art Flow later adds:
   - public profiles;
   - feeds;
   - comments;
   - likes;
   - direct messages;
   - public sharing/discovery;
   - mature-content categories.
